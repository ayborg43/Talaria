# Testing the Android app

## Automated (no phone needed)

```bash
cd android && export JAVA_HOME=/path/to/jdk17
./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest     # 125 tests: cost and digest wording, price parsing, bar scaling, agent-file share detection, clipboard truncation and the sensitive rule, task-notice cap and throttle, assistant rules; voice detection, server-address rules, the API client against a mock server, Make, pins, search, quick-action and alert helpers, phone-action argument checks and intent plans, @-mentions, the agent roster, the board widget's state
./gradlew :app:lintDebug             # no errors expected
```

## Real-phone checklist (cannot be done on an emulator)

1. **Microphone**: start a voice conversation in a quiet room, then with a fan or street noise. It should not start by itself, and should send after about a second of silence. Speak over a reply: it should stop talking.
2. **Alerts with the app closed**: turn on Notifications, swipe the app away, send yourself an approval. Repeat after the phone has been idle with the screen off for 30 minutes, and with battery saver on.
3. **Fingerprint / face**: turn on "Lock the app", leave for over a minute, come back.
4. **Real network**: sign in over https from outside your home network; try Wi-Fi off and on (the banner should say it cannot reach the server, then clear by itself).
5. **Takeover over mobile data**: open the browser view, scroll, type, pinch to zoom.
6. **Upload**: send a photo from the gallery and a PDF from Files; share a photo from another app.
7. **Quick access**: long-press the app icon (Talk, New chat, Approvals should be listed); add the widget and leave it for 30 minutes with the phone idle, then check it is still right; add the Talk tile and tap it from the lock screen (it should ask to unlock first); sign in with your password manager's fill, and see that it offers to save.
8. **Alerts that survive the night**: allow "Run in the background", leave the phone on a table with the screen off for a few hours, then start a task that needs an approval from another device: the notice should arrive within a minute (deep sleep can delay the live link; the quarter-hour check is the backstop). Repeat with battery saver on, and after restarting the phone without opening the app.
9. **Two servers**: sign in to both, make one ask for an approval while you are on the other; the notice should arrive within about 15 minutes and switch to that server when tapped.
10. **Phone tools**: switch each one on in Settings -> Your phone and ask Talaria from another device for each in turn ("where am I?", "what is Amina's number?", "what is on my calendar?", "what notifications do I have?", "how much battery is left?"). Then with the phone locked and the app closed, ask "where am I?" again (it should say it needs location allowed all the time until you allow that). Turn a switch off and confirm the agent is told it is not allowed. Check what Recently asked shows.
11. **Phone speech**: in Settings -> Voice leave "This phone" selected, tap the mic and say a sentence; it should show words as you speak and send when you pause. Try it in a noisy room, with a name you use often, in airplane mode (it should still work once the offline pack is downloaded), and with "Keep speech on this phone" turned off. Then pick "My server" and check talking over a reply still interrupts it.

12. **Model choice**: send a few short and long messages and look at which model each reply names. Turn on Smart routing and check a drafting request goes to a writing model. Give a few thumbs and use "Try a stronger model" on a reply that was weak, then look at "What it has learned so far". Time the first reply with Smart routing on and off.
13. **Events**: switch on Events, ask Talaria (from another device) "when I plug in my charger, tell me my first meeting tomorrow" and approve it; plug in the charger and wait for the answer. Try the battery level (let it drain, or use another device to set a level), your home Wi-Fi name (grant location when asked) and a Bluetooth device (your car or earbuds).
14. **Offline and photos**: turn on airplane mode, write a message (it should show as waiting), turn it off (it should arrive once). Take a photo from the + menu, the widget and the shortcut, and ask about it. Run your test questions on a new model, and search a folder of your own notes.
15. **Phone actions, board widget, agents**: switch each phone action on, ask Talaria for an alarm, a timer, a reminder, a place on the map and a text to a number you know. Each should show you exactly what it will do and wait, with no "Always" button. With Talaria open the clock should set an alarm or timer quietly and the calendar, maps and messaging apps should open filled in (the text still unsent). With Talaria closed and the screen off a notice should appear: tap it. Try one with its switch off. Add the board widget, leave it for an hour with the phone idle, then move a card in the web app and check the widget catches up within about 30 minutes. Chats sheet: your agents appear as contacts, and `@` in the message box offers their names.
16. **Cost and digest**: open Settings -> What the models cost after a day of use, set a price for your main model and see dollars appear; open While you were away, turn the schedule on for two minutes ahead and see the reply (and its notification with the app closed) arrive.
17. **Agent files**: share an agent from the Tasks screen to another phone, and share a `.agent.md` file to Talaria from a file manager and from a chat app (some apps send it as a generic file and will not list Talaria).
18. **Assistant**: choose Talaria as the Default digital assistant app, long-press the power button (or use the gesture), turn on "Use screenshot" and ask about a page in another app; check it opens quickly and nothing is sent until you press send. Check the Tile and shortcut for the clipboard, including with something copied from a password manager (it should refuse).
19. **Task notice**: start a long task from the web app, press Home and watch the notice change; press Stop on it; do it again with quiet hours on (no notice) and with the app lock on (no Stop button).
