# Testing the Android app

## Automated (no phone needed)

```bash
cd android && export JAVA_HOME=/path/to/jdk17
./gradlew :app:testDebugUnitTest     # 51 tests: voice detection, server-address rules, the API client against a mock server, Make, pins, search, quick-action and alert helpers
./gradlew :app:lintDebug             # no errors expected
```

## On an emulator or phone

Run a gateway you can sign in to (a throwaway password is fine), install `app-debug.apk`, and use `10.0.2.2:<port>` as the server address from the Android emulator.
Checked this way: sign-in, chat and Markdown, approvals (in the app and from a notification), uploads from "+" (photos, refused types), share-in,
session expiry, recipes, routine recording and running, conversation rename / archive / restore / delete, the live browser, voice (through the debug hook),
app lock, dark mode, large text, tablet layout, Make (every kind opens: PDF, page, slides, dashboard, document), saving a file to Downloads, Files screen, profile and notes (add, edit, forget), morning brief on / time / off, conversation search, pinning, plan card, the home-screen widget (placed on the launcher; quiet and "1 waiting" states; Ask opens the keyboard, New chat, Talk and the status chip open the right place; the status refreshed by itself after the question was cleared), launcher shortcuts and the quick-settings Talk tile (both open the app on the right action).
Phase 3 (alerts without Firebase): two local gateways and the emulator. Checked: the connection notice turning to "Cannot reach your server" with the network off and back to "Connected" within seconds of it returning (the wait is cut short by the network callback); a second server added, switched to, removed, and signing out of the live one moving to the other; the quarter-hour check (run on demand with `adb shell am broadcast -a app.talaria.DEBUG_WATCHDOG -n app.talaria/.DebugReceiver`) announcing a question waiting on the server that is not in use, and tapping it switching to that server and the question; an approval notice (cleaned text, Allow/Deny) removed when answered in the app; a reply typed into a notification reaching the agent; replies during quiet hours landing in the Silent section; the battery-allowance dialog and its "Allowed" status; a service started again by the system after an update.
Phone speech recognition (Android's own recognizer, now the default): checked on the emulator: the engine choice and the Settings row (Ready when the phone has an on-device recognizer), the recognizer starting when a conversation starts, error handling (no match listens again, a missing language pack makes Android offer its 62 MB English download and the app says so), heard words going through the same path as typed ones and the reply coming back, the recognizer being quiet while a reply is read and listening again after, and the server engine still working when chosen. NOT checked: real recognition (the emulator microphone is silent, and its offline pack would not finish installing), accuracy with your voice and accent, battery, and phones with no recognizer at all.
Phase 4 (phone tools): a fresh gateway and the emulator with a seeded contact, calendar events and notifications. Checked: every request refused with the phone not linked; the link coming up when one switch goes on and the server listing exactly what is on; a switched-off kind refused before it is sent; each of the five reads answering through the real agent loop (battery and network, location, contact by name, calendar, notifications); location refused with a clear reason in the background until "all the time" was allowed, then answered; contacts dropping from the list the moment its permission was revoked; the Recently asked list (shared and refused); the Read your phone rule set to Ask me producing an approval card. Not checked: a real GPS fix indoors, real contact books and calendars with many accounts, a phone asleep for hours (the link depends on the live connection from Phase 3).
Found while testing: Android does not allow a service to be started from an update broadcast (it does from a restart), so after an app update the live link returns when the app is opened; the quarter-hour check still announces waiting questions in between.

## Real-phone checklist (cannot be done on an emulator)

1. **Microphone**: start a voice conversation in a quiet room, then with a fan or street noise. It should not start by itself, and should send after about a second of silence. Speak over a reply: it should stop talking.
2. **Alerts with the app closed**: turn on Notifications, swipe the app away, send yourself an approval. Repeat after the phone has been idle with the screen off for 30 minutes, and with battery saver on.
3. **Fingerprint / face**: turn on "Lock the app", leave for over a minute, come back.
4. **Real network**: sign in over https from outside your home network; try Wi-Fi off and on (the banner should say it cannot reach the server, then clear by itself).
5. **Takeover over mobile data**: open the browser view, scroll, type, pinch to zoom.
6. **Upload**: send a photo from the gallery and a PDF from Files; share a photo from another app.
7. **Quick access**: long-press the app icon (Talk, New chat, Approvals should be listed); add the widget and leave it for 30 minutes with the phone idle, then check it is still right; add the Talk tile and tap it from the lock screen (it should ask to unlock first); sign in with your password manager's fill, and see that it offers to save.
8. **Alerts that survive the night**: allow "Run in the background", leave the phone on a table with the screen off for a few hours, then start a task that needs an approval from another device: the notice should arrive within a minute (deep sleep can delay the live link; the quarter-hour check is the backstop). Repeat with battery saver on, and after restarting the phone without opening the app.
9. **Two servers**: sign in to both, make one ask for an approval while you are on the other; the notice should arrive within about 15 minutes and switch to that server when tapped.
10. **Phone tools**: switch each one on in Settings -> Your phone and ask Talaria from another device for each in turn ("where am I?", "what is Amina's number?", "what is on my calendar?", "what notifications do I have?", "how much battery is left?"). Then with the phone locked and the app closed, ask "where am I?" again (it should say it needs location allowed all the time until you allow that). Turn a switch off and confirm the agent is told it is not allowed. Check what Recently asked shows.
11. **Phone speech**: in Settings -> Voice leave "This phone" selected, tap the mic and say a sentence; it should show words as you speak and send when you pause. Try it in a noisy room, with a name you use often, in airplane mode (it should still work once the offline pack is downloaded), and with "Keep speech on this phone" turned off. Then pick "My server" and check talking over a reply still interrupts it.
